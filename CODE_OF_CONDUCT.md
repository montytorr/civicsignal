# CivicSignal Code of Conduct

CivicSignal is civic infrastructure. Treat contributors, moderators, poll proposers, and participants with the seriousness that requires.

## Standards

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful, precise, and evidence-oriented.
- Critique ideas, implementation, wording, and methodology — not people.
- Keep poll wording neutral and publicly resolvable.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when proposing polls, sources, or governance changes.
- Protect private identity, security, and voting information.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or targeted abuse.
- Doxxing or attempts to reveal pseudonymous participants.
- Partisan campaigning disguised as poll design.
- Attempts to manipulate reputation, voting, resolution, or moderation.
- Public disclosure of vulnerabilities, secrets, or private user data before maintainers can respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove issues, pull requests, comments, poll proposals, or accounts that undermine safety, neutrality, privacy, or project integrity.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through GitHub private vulnerability reporting when security-sensitive, or email cal@dispofi.fr for non-security conduct concerns.
