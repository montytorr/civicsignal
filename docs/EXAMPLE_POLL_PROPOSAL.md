# Example poll proposal

Use this format when proposing a CivicSignal poll or reusable source template. Good proposals are neutral, publicly resolvable, and precise about what evidence counts.

## Question

Will the European Commission publish a formal proposal for a Digital Networks Act before 2026-12-31 23:59 CET?

## Topic and region

- **Topic:** Technology & AI
- **Region:** European Union

## Outcome options

- **Yes** — a formal European Commission proposal is published before the cutoff.
- **No** — no formal proposal is published before the cutoff.

## Voting cutoff

2026-06-30 23:59 CET.

## Resolution source

Primary source: European Commission press releases and official documents register.

Fallback source: EUR-Lex procedure or document entry if the Commission page is unavailable.

## Resolution criteria

Resolve **Yes** only if the European Commission publishes an official proposal, communication, or legislative package explicitly naming the Digital Networks Act, or the final official successor title if renamed in Commission materials.

Resolve **No** if the only evidence is media reporting, leaked drafts, speeches, consultation pages, stakeholder documents, or non-Commission publications.

Use the publication timestamp from the primary source where available. If only a date is available, treat publication as 12:00 CET on that date.

## Moderator notes

This is acceptable because it has a named public source, clear jurisdiction, clear deadline, and excludes leaks or speculation.
