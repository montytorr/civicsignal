# CivicSignal Governance

CivicSignal is operator-maintained during beta. Governance should become more public and reputation-aware only after the product has enough real users, resolved polls, and audit history to justify that move.

## Beta decision policy

Maintainers make final calls on:

- Security, authentication, and vote privacy.
- Reputation scoring and anti-Sybil controls.
- Poll moderation policy and source-of-truth standards.
- Resolution and dispute procedures.
- Public methodology, roadmap, and launch claims.

Material trust-model changes should ship with matching updates to the public methodology, roadmap, README, and relevant smoke checks.

## Poll governance

Polls should be:

- Neutral in wording.
- Publicly resolvable from named sources.
- Time-bounded with explicit cutoff and resolution windows.
- Clear about what counts and what does not.
- Rejected or sent back for changes when they depend on private data, vibes, advocacy, or subjective expert judgment.

## Trusted Panels

Trusted Panels are beta review surfaces, not constitutional bodies yet. Panel members help review evidence and disputes inside topic areas. Their decisions, rationale, and moderation notes should remain auditable.

## Future governance path

The intended direction is:

1. Operator-maintained beta with public methodology and issue discussion.
2. Trusted-panel review for disputes and source-quality edge cases.
3. Reputation-informed governance proposals once enough topic reputation exists.
4. Public-interest institutional APIs and governance processes after repeatable audit/export tooling exists.

No tradable token, pay-to-influence voting, or transferable reputation belongs in CivicSignal governance.
